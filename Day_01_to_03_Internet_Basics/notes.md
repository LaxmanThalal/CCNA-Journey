\# 🚀 Day 1 to 3: Fundamentals of Computer Networks \& Physical Media



\## 📅 Day 1: Core Networking Concepts

\* \*\*What is a Computer Network?\*\* Understanding interconnected systems and their purpose.

\* \*\*Protocols:\*\* Why we need standard protocols for seamless communication.

\* \*\*Switching \& Routing:\*\* How data moves within a local network (Switching) and across multiple networks (Routing).

\* \*\*Message Transmission:\*\* Deep dive into how computers package and transmit messages across a network.



\## 📅 Day 2: Network Media, Devices \& Topologies

\* \*\*Transmission Media \& Technologies:\*\* Exploring physical and logical layer cable technologies.

\* \*\*Network Devices:\*\* Understanding the role and differences between Hubs, Repeaters, and Bridges.

\* \*\*Topologies:\*\* Analyzing various network structures and their advantages/disadvantages.
